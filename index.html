About

I like the part of engineering where hardware discipline meets machine intelligence.

I graduated from Birzeit University in July 2026 with a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, emphasis in Intelligent Systems. Most of my work is in AI: emotion recognition from speech and faces, multimodal fusion, transfer learning for vision, retrieval and NLP.

I also come from silicon. I spent a summer as a Design Verification Intern at Orion VLSI Technologies, built a pipelined RISC processor in SystemVerilog, and wrote UVM testbenches that hit 100% functional coverage. That habit carries over to ML: measure everything, validate on data the model never saw, and check what it is actually looking at.

I also teach and organize. I ran live Verilog walkthroughs for four semesters and have logged 505 volunteer hours at Birzeit.
